## 5. Before engaging LaunchUNC (or any program)

1. **Read UNC's IP / copyright policy.** As a student using no substantial university
   resources, you likely retain ownership — but confirm in writing.
2. **Read the LaunchUNC participation agreement** *before signing*. Check specifically
   for: equity taken, any IP license/assignment to the program or university, and
   affiliation requirements. Flag anything that touches ownership to your attorney.
3. **Watch public disclosure.** A public demo day can start or forfeit patent clocks.
   Keep the method non-enabling in anything public.
4. **NDAs.** Most investors won't sign one — that's fine, because you only disclose the
   vision, never the trade-secret method.
